The 5C Check

Pause before you use an AI translation

  1. 1

    Context

    What is happening?

  2. 2

    Check

    Is the meaning accurate?

  3. 3

    Culture

    What might have disappeared?

  4. 4

    Confidentiality

    Should I give this information to AI?

  5. 5

    Create

    You make the final decision

Worked example

One phrase, several possibilities

加油 → “Add oil!”

Depending on the situation, a contextual translation could be:

  • “Come on!”
  • “Keep going!”
  • “You can do it!”

Classroom challenge

What would you decide?

老师，辛苦了。
