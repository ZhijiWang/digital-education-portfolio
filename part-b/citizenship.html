Retrofitted Part A resource · Year 7–8 Chinese

Can AI Translate This?

The same Chinese phrase can mean different things in English. Read the situation before you choose.

View the original Part A version · See what changed in the UDL retrofit

Safe use: Work with the imaginary classroom examples below. Keep names, private messages and login details out of AI tools. This page does not connect to an AI service.

Step 1 · Compare contexts

One Chinese phrase, different English

Start with 不好意思. Notice what changes in these three conversations.

Apologising

不好意思，我迟到了。

Bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ chídào le.

“Sorry, I'm late.”

Getting attention

不好意思，请问洗手间在哪里？

Bù hǎoyìsi, qǐngwèn xǐshǒujiān zài nǎlǐ?

“Excuse me, where is the bathroom?”

Asking a favour

真不好意思，又麻烦你了。

Zhēn bù hǎoyìsi, yòu máfan nǐ le.

“Sorry to bother you again.”

Three things to check before choosing
  1. Who is speaking, and to whom?
  2. What is the speaker trying to do?
  3. Which English version fits that moment?
