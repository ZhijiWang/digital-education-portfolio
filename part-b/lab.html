Student task · Year 7–8 Chinese

Translation Evidence Lab

Here are two Chinese school messages and English translations that almost fit. Check the details, then write the English you would use.

Before you begin: Use the classroom examples here. Do not enter names, personal messages or passwords into AI tools. This activity works without an AI account.

Learning intention

I can check an English translation against the Chinese message, the situation and a source I can name.

Success criteria

  • I can explain what I would check before trusting Source A.
  • I can compare a suggested translation with the situation and a named source.
  • I can write my own English and use a detail from the situation to explain it.

Need a clue? Questions to ask

What does the Chinese actually say? Has the action already happened? Which source gives a word meaning, and which one tells you what is happening? The sources may disagree.
