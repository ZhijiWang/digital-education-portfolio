Student task · Year 7–8 Chinese

Translation Evidence Lab

An AI translation can sound convincing. Can you check it against the original message and its context?

Before you begin: Use the classroom examples here. Do not enter names, personal messages or passwords into AI tools. This activity works without an AI account.

Learning intention

I can evaluate a digital translation claim using the original Chinese, the speaker's context, and a source I can identify.

Success criteria

  • I can identify what an AI-style suggestion can and cannot prove.
  • I can check a phrase with a named source and record what I found or still need to check.
  • I can revise the English and explain my decision using evidence about the audience.

Check beyond the example

The source cards were written by the teacher. Check the word 麻烦 in a second source. A dictionary can clarify a word's meanings; the original message still tells you how it is being used.

Need a clue? Questions to ask

Has the person already helped? Who is being addressed? Which source tells you about the speaker's purpose? You may use a school-approved dictionary or ask your teacher.
