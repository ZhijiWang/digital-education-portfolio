Languages, Technologies and Critical Digital Literacy

I am a pre-service secondary teacher with teaching interests in Languages and Technologies. My experience learning and using different languages has made me particularly interested in the relationship between language, culture and digital communication. This portfolio documents how I am developing that interest into classroom practice.

Digital technologies are now part of how students communicate, research, translate and create. I see digital literacy as the ability to make informed decisions about these tools: to recognise when they are useful, question what they produce, protect personal information, and know when human judgement is needed.

This is especially important with generative AI. In a language classroom, an AI tool can produce a fluent translation within seconds, but fluency does not guarantee that tone, context or cultural meaning has been understood. I want students to become active users of technology who can check, question and revise what a digital system gives them.

Across this portfolio, I explore practical ways to use digital technologies while maintaining student agency, safety and responsibility. The focus is on resources that I could realistically adapt and use in my own secondary classroom.
